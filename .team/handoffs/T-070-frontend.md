## Known gaps or risks
- Backend gaps (contract has no answer; nothing invented): (1) The page cannot tell the exact trial effect from the server: it computes it from `days[].cookedAt` with `freeTrialEffect`, like the customer page does. A `trialEffect` field on the cancelled/no-show booking would make it exact. (2) No `isActive` per day, so "visits that have not happened were freed" is stated generally. (3) `cancellationTiming` for the confirm step is computed in the browser from the first day (same rule as the server, `isInsideLateWindow`); the server value is shown after the cancel. (4) The contract says no-show is reportable "from the visit date" because the model has no clock time; a chef can report at 00:01 on day 1 before the customer could be expected.
- T-066 (parallel): nothing here depends on it. Wording kept neutral: nothing says what an auto-completed booking does. The no-show text covers only a customer no-show (trial counts as used, already built). The chef-cancel trial text follows D-26 as built (any cooked day uses the trial).
- A stale error may show again if the chef closes and reopens a confirm step after a failure (the section hook has no reset); minor.
- Customer no-show of the chef (customer side) and "report a problem" are not built (not in the Work Order).
